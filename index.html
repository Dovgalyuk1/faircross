    02 — HOW IT WORKS

    Four steps, none of them public.

    Everything a sniper normally reads before it acts, the side, the size and the price, stays sealed until the moment it stops mattering.

    01

    Seal

    You pick the token, the size and the most you are willing to pay. The order is sealed in your browser before it leaves, so the chain learns that an order exists and absolutely nothing else about it.

    02

    Collect

    The window stays open for five minutes and quietly collects every sealed order that shows up. There is no book to read and no queue to jump, so sending your transaction faster buys you exactly nothing.

    03

    Cross

    When the window closes, every order opens at once and the venue finds the single price where the most volume trades. Everyone who is in, from the first bot to the last human, fills at that price.

    04

    Settle

    Fills and leftovers land back in your balance right after the cross. Anything that did not fill either rolls into the next window or comes home to you, whichever you chose when you sealed it.

    05 — ORDER TYPES

    Six ways to stay quiet.

    Every type below goes through the same window and fills at the same clearing price. They only change how much of you shows up, and when.

    Standard

    STD

    Fills at the clearing price as long as the clearing price is under your cap. Whatever does not fill comes back to you after the window settles, untouched and still unseen.

    Split

    SPLIT 2–12

    Cuts one order into equal parts across two to twelve windows. Useful when you want in, but not all at once, and definitely not in a way anyone can see coming.

    Roll

    ROLL

    Keeps the unfilled part sealed and carries it into the next windows, up to twelve in a row. It never shows up anywhere in between, not even as a rumour.

    All or none

    AON

    Fills completely at the cross or does not fill at all. Built for people who have already made a decision and have no interest in owning half of it.

    Late

    NEXT

    Arrives while a window is already closing and joins the next one without a word. Being late costs exactly what being early costs, which is the entire idea of this place.

    Block

    BLOCK

    A large order that crosses whole against a matching counter-order at the clearing price. It still waits in the window like everyone else, size does not buy a side door.
